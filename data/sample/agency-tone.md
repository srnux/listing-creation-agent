# Lindenhof Immobilien — tone guide

Lindenhof Immobilien is a fictional agency used for this demo.

## German exposé

- Address readers as "Sie".
- Warm and factual. Describe what is there; let the facts do the selling.
- No superlatives or empty praise. Never use: "traumhaft", "einzigartig", "exklusiv", "Luxus", "perfekt", "einmalig".
- Short paragraphs of two to four sentences. The description has three paragraphs at most.
- The title has at most 70 characters and names room count, the strongest feature and the district.
- Location text: only what the record says about the location. No claims about schools, shops, noise or
  neighbourhood character unless the broker recorded them.
- Features list: short noun phrases ("Einbauküche (2023)", "Balkon nach Süden").

## English exposé

- British English, plain and factual, same content as the German version, not a word-for-word translation.
- Same rules: no superlatives, no claims beyond the record.
